# Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.1

1.21.1 es una revisión a Bedrock Edition lanzada el 20 de junio de 2024, la cual corrige dos errores.

## Notas de la versión

# Fixes:

- Fixed a hardlock that could occur when playing splitscreen multiplayer on PlayStation®4- Fixed a bug where players with a freshly installed Minecraft needed to restart before being able to see Realms Stories
