# Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.22

1.21.22 es una revisión de Bedrock Edition lanzada el 26 de agosto de 2024 para todas las plataformas excepto iOS, iPadOS, y Fire, 27 de agosto para iOS, iPadOS, and Fire, la cual corrige errores.

## Notas de la versión

A new update has been released to address a few issues with the 1.21.20 release.
# Fixes:

- Fixed several crashes that could occur during gameplay- Improved loading of add-ons from Marketplace- Fixed a bug where all custom blocks were rotated 180 degrees in the UI
